Dhaka, Bangladesh

Dhaka is the capital city of Bangladesh.

It is one of the largest and most important cities in the country.

Dhaka is located near the Buriganga River.

The city is an important center for business, education, culture, and government.

Dhaka is also known for its historic buildings, busy markets, and large number of rickshaws.
